# Code of Conduct

`actdata-plugins` is maintained as part of Patterson Companies' internal engineering practice.
Contributors are expected to behave consistently with Patterson's own employee standards of
conduct, whether they are Patterson personnel, contractors, or agents acting on someone's behalf.

This document adapts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) for a B2B
internal engineering context.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a good environment:

- Giving and receiving review feedback on the work, not the person.
- Stating uncertainty plainly. "I am not sure this is what the standard says" is more useful than
  a confident guess.
- Recording a gap rather than filling it. When a source is silent, write `[TBD: what is missing]`
  and escalate to whoever owns it.
- Assuming a colleague's odd-looking change had a reason, and asking what it was.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discriminatory language, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without explicit permission.
- **Manufacturing organizational policy.** Do not assert that ACT Data or Patterson requires
  something the source material does not actually say, and do not remove a documented `[TBD]`
  without the authority to resolve it. A plugin here is read by agents as authoritative; an
  invented requirement propagates into other people's work as though it were real.
- **Committing content that misrepresents what a plugin does.** A README that claims a validator
  certifies compliance, or a skill description that overstates its coverage, is a correctness
  problem with a conduct dimension — it causes other people to rely on something that is not true.

## Scope

This applies to all repository spaces — issues, pull requests, commit messages, and code review —
and to any setting where someone is representing this project or Patterson Companies.

## Enforcement

Report a concern to the repository's listed [`CODEOWNERS`](CODEOWNERS) team, or through Patterson's
internal HR and compliance channels for matters involving Patterson personnel. Reports are handled
discreetly.
